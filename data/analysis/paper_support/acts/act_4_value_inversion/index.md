## Synthesis

The evidence does not demonstrate that SDD/BDUF is superior to Agile, BDUF is universally appropriate, or explicit planning causes better outcomes. The defensible contribution is a proposed educational and methodological response: future studies can test whether specification quality, coordination checkpoints, and AI-process assessment improve outcomes under an appropriate comparative design. This act converts the evidence boundary into a research agenda, not into a positive result.
