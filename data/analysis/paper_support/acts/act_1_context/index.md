## Synthesis

The educational setting makes planning, specification, and coordination important questions under AI-assisted programming, but the current evidence does not test Act 1 as an outcome. The manuscript should use this act to motivate the study and its teaching relevance, then state the empirical boundary plainly. Any SDD/BDUF implication belongs to the proposed level and requires future evaluation.
