Overview

Browse three research questions about perceptions, activity, and coordination in student projects using generative AI. These are descriptive and exploratory observations, not causal evidence or a ranking of teams.

  • RQ1: Self-report and coded perceptions

    M1-M2: reported AI use, coded perceptions, and role perceptions. Survey summaries do not measure actual AI-use telemetry.

  • RQ2: Repository activity and transcript evidence

    M3-M5: participation, code change, and lexical aggregates. Repository and transcript evidence have different analytical grains.

  • RQ3: Planning, inactivity, rework, and associations

    M6-M9: structural and content evidence, timing, rework eligibility, and published exploratory associations.

Understand the evidence grain

Each dataset has a specific unit of observation. Similar labels do not make datasets interchangeable or authorize joining them.

Survey summaries
Aggregated by cohort and checkpoint, with role or category where documented. There is no team key; responses cannot be assigned to project teams.
Repository activity
Team-semesters are identified by cohort plus team, not by the repeated team label alone. Checkpoint and window keys must also be retained where present. Coverage spans 14 team-semesters: nine in 2025.2 and five in 2026.1.
Transcript lexical aggregates
One measured 2025.2 corpus, summarized by checkpoint, not individual teams. No transcript measurements are available for 2026.1.
Published summaries and associations
Fixed published populations and original sample sizes. Estimates are not recalculated for a selected subset.

Missing is not zero. Unavailable measurement, ineligible observation, an empty selection, and a failed load are different states.

Explore

Interactive dataset selection is not implemented yet. The research-question guide below describes the available evidence. The public dataset catalog (JSON) provides the full field definitions and relative paths to approved JSON and CSV files.

RQ1: Self-report and coded perceptions

  • M1: AI use and perceptions. Frequency, tasks, tools, experience, autonomy, career topics, and coded perception families.
  • M2: Role perceptions. Role-specific distributions and summaries; paired-response sensitivity evidence is labeled separately.

Use cohort, checkpoint, role, or category only where supported. These are survey aggregates, never team-level observations. Keep original respondent, paired-response, and topic-assignment denominators distinct.

RQ2: Repository activity and transcript evidence

  • M3: Participation and concentration. Observed commits, author concentration, and explicitly labeled activity windows.
  • M4: Code change. Checkpoint churn and artifact composition; clean change is distinct from all change.
  • M5: Lexical aggregates. Marker density, composition, corpus coverage, and lexical definitions from the measured transcript corpus.

Repository comparisons use composite team-semester keys. Rolling windows overlap; non-overlapping phase bins are a separate view. Global transcript aggregates must not be correlated with individual teams.

RQ3: Planning, inactivity, rework, and associations

  • M6: Planning evidence. M6a structural artifacts and M6b structured content. Content was measured for nine cases; five are unavailable, not cases with zero planning evidence.
  • M7: Inactivity. Checkpoint timing diagnostics, not evidence of planning omission.
  • M8: Rework. Magnitude and baseline-eligible ratios; 12 of 14 team-semesters have an observed baseline.
  • M9: Exploratory associations. Published predictor/outcome rows, fixed eligibility strata, and leave-one-out diagnostics.

Supporting complexity, regularity, planning, and timing profiles preserve their original definitions and thresholds. Do not compute new subset correlations or interpret diagnostics as causal effects.

Measures and methods

Read the selected dataset's units, denominator, coverage, and eligibility before comparing values. The catalog contains the complete dataset-specific definitions.

Scales, eligibility, and interpretation

Perception scales
M1 coded perception scores use 0-4. Raw autonomy categories and M2 Likert responses use 1-5; these scales are not interchangeable.
Unavailable and empty evidence
Unmeasured planning-content fields are null, not zero. Successfully measured empty evidence can be an observed zero. Absent rows are not synthesized.
Rework eligibility
Ratios require an observed baseline and a nonzero clean-churn denominator. Ineligible ratios are null with an explicit status. Rework is not a defect count.
Fixed estimates
Pooled summaries, association estimates, thresholds, and original sample sizes remain fixed. Leave-one-out diagnostics are not confidence intervals.
Exact values
Approved JSON and CSV files preserve the same rows, columns, and scientific precision. Empty CSV cells represent null values, not numeric zero.
