\section{Threats to Validity}
The constructs rely on complementary but imperfect proxies. M1 and M2 are
survey measures, not behavioral telemetry; M6a and M6b capture
repository-visible artifacts and commit subjects, not architectural quality or
planning intent; M8 uses file-path provenance rather than semantic defect
validation; and M5 uses lexical markers rather than direct friction diagnoses.
These boundaries prevent the metrics from being treated as interchangeable
measures of quality, effort, or productivity.

The observational design constrains causal and external validity. Late-stage
repository concentration remains compatible with ordinary deadline pressure
and ``student syndrome.'' We mitigate this threat with internal contrasts by
score change, planning scope, non-overlapping phases, and regularity proxies,
but without a non-AI control group or timestamped AI-use telemetry we cannot
separate GenAI-related debt, conventional procrastination, process choice, and
project difficulty. Rolling windows overlap, Git cannot observe off-repository
work, and teams freely chose Scrum, Kanban, other Agile variants, or unguided
approaches; regularity summaries are descriptive Git proxies, not validated
process-quality measures.

Small-sample and coverage limits are substantial. RQ3 covers 14
team-semesters in one educational setting; leave-one-out diagnostics make
case influence visible but are not confidence intervals. Analytical grains
differ across student responses, team-semesters, rolling windows, and the
global transcript corpus, so denominators cannot be pooled implicitly. M5
transcripts are available only for 2025.2; 2026.1 is therefore unavailable,
not zero evidence. Five M6b cases lacked T1 commit subjects and were not
imputed.

Finally, project complexity remains a plausible confound. The course required
an integrated GenAI component but did not mandate RAG or a common architecture,
and none of the 14 projects implemented a full RAG pipeline. Teams'
self-selected AI integrations differed in technical complexity. Complexity
profiles and stratified summaries mitigate this threat descriptively, but
cannot isolate causal effects. Likewise, SDD is discussed as a theoretically
motivated intervention hypothesis; this study neither implements nor evaluates
SDD.
