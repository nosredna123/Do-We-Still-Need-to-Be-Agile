## Planned use

Background will use the academic sources for claims about GenAI, collaboration, education, architecture, and technical debt. GitHub and Thoughtworks will define contemporary SDD practice only. Methodology will cite Runeson/Stol for empirical framing and standard statistical sources for Spearman correlation, descriptive summaries, and leave-one-out sensitivity. No source will be used to claim that SDD improves outcomes unless an empirical comparative study supports that claim.
